CAD collision fix

Root cause: inward f64-to-f32 rounding at zero gap. The old adapter reserved no precision guard and eroded outlines during its overlap check. A four-point rectangle reproduces the real overlap; the actual four-point windbreaker under-panel also loses coverage without simplification.

The fix cleans outlines, rejects crossings, guards every collision shape, checks full-polygon coverage, and repairs final contacts. Every returned marker and progress improvement passes the independent validator.
