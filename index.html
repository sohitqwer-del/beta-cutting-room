Features

What BETA does today

Everything runs on your own computer. The heavy work (geometry, nesting, checks, files) is written in Rust for speed.

Pattern

  • Seven editable basic blocks or a blank sheet, on a drafting canvas
  • Size-chart grading, all sizes stacked, seam checks
  • Spec sheet: points of measure per size (PDF, Excel)
  • DXF-AAMA, plotter PLT and 1:1 PDF out; DXF-AAMA and a Seamly2D subset in

Marker

  • Automatic nesting with grain, one-way nap, left/right pairs, ratios and gaps
  • The marker improves live on screen; stop and keep the best
  • Improve one area by hand: preview, apply, undo New in 0.8
  • Keeps your previous marker when it is shorter and still valid New in 0.8
  • Every marker checked by an independent validator; plotter, DXF, PDF and cutter files

Cutting room

  • Cut plans: ratios, plies, passes and fabric for an order
  • Compare fabric widths, ratios and the cost of the whole order New in 0.8
  • Rolls: 4-point inspection, shade and shrinkage groups
  • Spreading sheets, bundles, QR tickets, re-cuts and the sewing hand-off

Small factories

  • Photo to marker on a phone, with an A4 sheet as the scale
  • Reusable paper pattern sets with a tape check New in 0.8
  • Fabric groups (main, lining, rib, fusing) with GSM, one marker each New in 0.8
  • Full-size tiles, chalk guide and projector mode; works offline as an app

Not yet, or not proven

We prefer to tell you before you find out.

  • The 0.8 additions (area improvement, order-cost comparison, paper pattern sets) are new and tested only in parts.
  • Basic blocks pass 10,806 automatic checks, but they need sewn samples and a fit review by a pattern master.
  • Folded and tube lays use more fabric than open lays on many orders. The app shows the real yield so you can see it.
  • Real-photo accuracy of Photo to marker is not yet measured in factories. Check sizes against the paper before cutting.
  • Plotter and cutter profiles are generic. Test them on your machines before production.
  • The browser plans on one thread (about 2 seconds per marker). Big jobs are much better in the desktop app.
  • The Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning. Hindi and Tamil are drafts.

Method, assumptions and all results →

Folded fabric is still behind open fabric

In our 0.7 tests, folded T-shirt orders with odd counts per size needed 23% to 74% more fabric than an open lay, and tubes about 11% to 12% more. 0.8 tries mixed fold choices per piece: in a small pilot on 12 public jobs the change ranged from 1.9% more fabric (folded hoodie) to 5.7% less (shirt with a locked piece); the folded T-shirt did not change. The app shows the real yield and the pieces cut but not used, so you can decide.

Privacy

Your files stay on your device

BETA runs in your browser, in the desktop app, or on a factory server you choose. There is no BETA cloud.

  • Patterns, markers, orders and roll data stay in this browser or on your own PCs and server.
  • No account, no tracking, no usage data and no crash reports sent anywhere. This website has no cookies.
  • Works without internet once installed or opened once. Keep backup files of your work.
  • Feedback is your choice. GitHub issues are public, so do not attach factory files there.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is it free?

Yes. The code is Apache-2.0 and there is no licence fee. Your own computers, hosting, printing and any training or support you choose to buy still cost money.

Is it open source?

Yes, all of it, under Apache-2.0. Third-party code and data keep their own licences; see the notices.

Who built it?

Sohit and contributors, with the help of modern AI coding tools. It is a young project: it needs review by real pattern masters, cutting masters and factory owners.

Can I trust the results?

An independent validator and many tests check every marker, but they do not replace your own checks. Before production, check the scale, the fit, the ply and layer counts and the files on your plotter or cutter.

Can it replace my commercial CAD?

Not proven. We make no general claim that BETA is better. On the public Lectra benchmark the results are mixed: see the numbers.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your device: in this browser, in the desktop app, or on a factory server you choose. There is no account and no analytics. If you attach files to a public GitHub issue, those files become public.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The desktop app is fully local. The browser app works offline after its first complete visit (it keeps an offline copy). Save backup files of your work either way.

How good is the nesting?

Close to a commercial engine on some jobs and behind on others. On public Lectra tasks, against Lectra's recorded results: with 60 seconds per task (300 tasks, measured 2026-10-28): 1.39 points less efficient on average, median 1.57 points less efficient; 77 wins, 12 ties, 211 losses. Historical 20 October, with Lectra's own time per task (60 tasks, measured 2026-10-19): 0.42 points more efficient on average, median 0.30 points less efficient; 25 wins, 3 ties, 32 losses. See the benchmark for the method.
