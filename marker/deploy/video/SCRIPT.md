## Walkthrough (three paths)

- **home**: BETA Cutting Room is free, open-source software for garment factories. It runs in your browser, with no account. This is the real browser app, using only synthetic sample patterns. Let us follow three ways to start.
- **photo**: First, a small unit with paper patterns. Open Photo to marker. Photograph your pieces beside an A four sheet on a flat surface. Here we load an original synthetic test image through the real photo reader, not the practice button.
- **review**: Review each outline. Give it a name and size. Check copies, pairs, fabric group and grain. Keep main fabric, lining, rib and fusing separate. They can each have their own fabric settings.
- **tape**: Before cutting real cloth, check a distance against your paper pattern with a tape. Choose two points, enter the measured distance and review the difference. A clean test image does not prove accuracy on factory photographs.
- **paperMarker**: Set the fabric width and how many garments you need. Make the marker. The real nesting engine places the accepted pieces across the fabric. Review the result before printing, and save your pattern set for the next order.
- **pattern**: Second, the pattern master. This sample tour starts from the T-shirt block. The pattern is a starting draft, not a promise of fit. Edit the style for your customer, then sew a sample before production.
- **grade**: Grade from the size chart. Here the same style has small, medium, large and extra large sizes. Review the graded outlines and seam checks. Changing a measurement changes the draft, so check your work again after editing.
- **lay**: Third, the cutting room. Choose the fabric width and lay. Set layers, face direction and garment rules. Open, folded and tube fabric need different treatment. Gaps and stripe matching also affect how the pieces can fit.
- **marker**: Now make the marker. You are watching the actual search, at its recorded speed. The layout updates as a better result arrives. You can stop and keep the best result so far. Review the length, fabric use and validation before cutting.
- **cut**: The cut view shows marker length, layers, passes and fabric needed for this sample. Use the cut plan for the whole order. From there, continue to rolls, spreading sheets, bundles and tickets as your cutting room needs.
- **files**: Export plotter files, DXF and full-size or tiled PDF files from the browser. Here we actually download three sample files. Test a small file on your own plotter first. These files do not prove support for every cutter or machine.
- **limits**: BETA is young and not perfect. It does not establish a general win over commercial CAD. Folded and tube jobs still need care. Real photo accuracy, fit and machine compatibility need more factory testing. Always check a sample before production.
- **feedback**: Help us improve it. Open Feedback and describe what went wrong, what you expected and the steps to repeat it. Share files only if you have permission. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Try the sample tour on the website.
